## How Product Uses This Repo

### Briefs
- Written in Google Docs first. The Google Doc is the living source of truth.
- CONTEXT.md in each project folder captures a point-in-time snapshot with links to the source doc.
- Briefs explain the why and the what. Start from user psychology, not feature lists.
- Keep it tight — 1-2 pages for kickoff briefs.

### PRDs
- PRDs describe behavior and conditional logic. Do NOT describe UI details visible in Figma.
- Do NOT include success metrics, analytics events, or open questions in PRDs — those have their own files.
- Cross-check CONTEXT.md against the PRD before declaring complete.

### Leadership Updates
- Focus on clean text and tables — no fancy formatting.
- Tone should be collaborative, not adversarial between teams.

### Weekly Meeting Prep
- Workflow in `meetings/weekly/CLAUDE.md`. Trigger: "write top of mind for [date]"
- Scans Slack channels, meeting notes, roadmap, and calendar.

### Roadmap Updates
- Verify statuses against Slack and meeting notes before writing to the spreadsheet.
- Always show proposed changes for approval before writing.
- Do not assume launch dates — confirm from primary sources.

### Review Feedback (review-taste.md)
- After any brief/design/ship review, capture recurring feedback patterns in `product/review-taste.md`.
- Track: reviewer-specific patterns, recurring themes, principles that emerge over multiple reviews.
- This builds institutional knowledge about what leadership cares about, so future deliverables are better the first time.
