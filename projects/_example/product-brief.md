## Problem

Merchants invest in marketing campaigns but have no way to see the return at a glance. The analytics dashboard shows total revenue as a single number. A merchant spending $5K/month on marketing can't tell if those campaigns drove $50K in revenue or $5K. This uncertainty leads to:

- 15+ support tickets per month asking "is my marketing working?"
- Campaign cancellations driven by anxiety, not data
- Merchants defaulting to the cheapest campaign instead of the most effective one

## Goals

- Give merchants a clear, immediate answer to "is my marketing spend working?"
- Reduce marketing-related support tickets by 50%
- Increase campaign retention (fewer cancellations in the first 30 days)

## Requirements

### Must Have

- Revenue breakdown: organic vs marketing-driven, displayed as a stacked bar chart
- Time range selector: 90d (default), 30d, 7d, custom
- Campaign attribution: which campaign drove which marketing orders
- Mobile-responsive (60% of merchants check analytics on mobile)

### Nice to Have

- Drill-down from chart segment to individual orders
- Export to CSV
- Comparison view (this period vs last period)

## Success Metrics

- Support ticket volume for "is marketing working?" category drops by 50%
- Campaign cancellation rate in first 30 days decreases
- Dashboard page engagement (time on page, drill-down rate) increases
