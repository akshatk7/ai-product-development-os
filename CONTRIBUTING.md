# Contributing

**This repo is a template, not a collaborative project.**

You are not meant to contribute to this repo directly. Instead:

1. **Fork** this repo (click "Fork" in the top right on GitHub)
2. **Rename** it to something that fits your team (e.g., `my-team-product-os`)
3. **Customize** it by following `QUICKSTART.md` and `CUSTOMIZE.md`
4. **Make all your changes in your fork** — that's your team's living knowledge base

Pull requests and direct commits to this repo will not be accepted (except from the maintainer). This is intentional — your team's instance should diverge and evolve independently.

## Questions or Feedback?

If you have ideas for improving the template itself (not your team's instance), [open an issue](https://github.com/akshatk7/ai-product-development-os/issues). Issues are welcome for template feedback — PRs are not.
